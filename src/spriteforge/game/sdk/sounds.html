Sound editor

Open the game

Every sound is synthesised by the game itself (no audio files). Press ▶ to hear a sound, change its preset or the quick tweaks, or open its layers to shape it. A sound plays on the moments in plays on (comma separated); a loop repeats while its moment lasts (move: while the player moves). Saving here makes a new locked version of the sounds; reload the game to hear it. In the game, M turns the sound on or off.
