Describe tile roles, collision rules, encounter spacing, and exit reachability in typed artifacts. Keep changes scoped to the requested level or tile ids. With the `library.*` tools, `library_tiles(camera)` lists installed tile sets, their named tiles, variants (e.g. autumn, winter) with trigger words, and parallax backdrops.
